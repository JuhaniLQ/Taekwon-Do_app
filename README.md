## ITF Taekwon-Do Training Support App

**This app is designed to support your ITF Taekwon-Do training.**  
Use it to **review and recall techniques** you have already learned, *not* as a tool for learning new ones.

### Main Features
- Browse technique, pattern, crushing, special and sparring videos
- Filter content with belt-level and category
- Adjust video playback speed and pin technique videos for comparison
- Learn Korean words with interactive body diagrams
- Study Taekwon-Do theory and fundamentals
- Test your knowledge with quizzes on techniques, body parts and terminology
- Download and store content locally for offline use 
- Update content in-app from the cloud :globe_with_meridians:
